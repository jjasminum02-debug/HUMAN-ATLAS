# T65 source review notes

Access date: 2026-10-02. The linked pages were opened as full text in the web reader during this task. The page bytes were not downloaded or archived; no page-byte hash is asserted. The hash pinned by the T65 data is the SHA-256 of this local review-note file, not the source page.

## Fibular nerve learning context

- Kim et al., “Branching Patterns and Anatomical Course of the Common Fibular Nerve,” 2019, DOI 10.5535/arm.2019.43.6.700, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6960080/. Opened full text, Results and Discussion, lines 107–123 and 206–213. The paper describes the nerve course around the fibular head and branching variation; it does not establish one universal course or a patient-specific compression site.
- Shekhawat et al., “Anatomical study of fascial and aponeurotic bands in the anterolateral leg,” Anatomy & Cell Biology 58(3), 2025, DOI 10.5115/acb.24.273, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12519015/. Opened full text, Results/Discussion, lines 186–209 and 295–304. The cadaveric paper describes variable fascial/aponeurotic relations and possible anatomic compression contexts; it does not validate a clinical diagnosis from the model.
- “Foot Drop: An Anatomical, Clinical, and Electrodiagnostic Approach to Localization,” 2026, DOI 10.26502/fjsrs00101, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13591402/. Opened full text, lines 72–95. Used only for general branch-function distinctions (deep branch with dorsiflexion/toe extension; superficial branch with eversion and dorsal-foot sensory territory). No diagnostic or treatment instruction is projected.

## Muscle attachment learning context

- StatPearls, “Anatomy, Shoulder and Upper Limb, Arm Abductor Muscles,” NBK537148, updated 2023-05-04, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537148/. Opened full text, “Structure and Function” / deltoid and trapezius attachments, lines 96–102. Used for the exact clavicular/acromial/scapular-spinal deltoid parts and descending/transverse/ascending trapezius attachment distinctions.
- StatPearls, “Anatomy, Back, Extrinsic Muscles,” NBK537216, updated 2024-01-30, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537216/. Opened full text, “Structure and Function,” lines 63–68. Used for latissimus dorsi and rhomboid attachment summaries and trapezius part scope.
- StatPearls, “Anatomy, Back, Rhomboid Muscles,” NBK534856, updated 2023-05-16, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK534856/. Opened full text, “Structure and Function,” lines 53–55. Cross-checks the distinct rhomboid minor and major source extents.

## Access and scope limits

NCBI pages were not downloaded as local HTML in this task. Their source-page SHA-256 values are therefore null. Exact URLs, edition/update metadata, and field locators are stored in `source-review.json`; the opened-text reviewer notes above are hash-pinned. The learner summaries are concise Korean paraphrases, not direct quotations. No source-level raw excerpt is included in the learner bundle.

The upper-arm biceps part records were not added: an exact source candidate appeared in search results, but a directly opened source page with its part-specific field locator was not available in the required evidence path during this task. Search-index text alone was not accepted as field evidence.
